**Version:** 2026-09-05

2. **Code ownership.** Generated application code belongs to the customer organization. Loop-it does not claim copyright in that output.
3. **Isolation is not optional.** Sandbox, egress, gates, audit, and budgets (I1–I12) remain in force even when the model catalogue is unrestricted.
4. **Acceptable use.** Use is subject to `docs/legal/aup.md`. Infrastructure abuse can suspend a tenant without changing model-content policy.
5. **Credits.** Spend is metered on the Loop-it ledger (`services/billing`). Stripe, when configured, is a payment rail only; the ledger is source of truth.
6. **No warranty for generated apps.** Operators and customers review, test, and deploy their own artifacts. Preview URLs are authenticated; production deploys require an I7 confirmation gate.
7. **Data.** Control-plane data handling is described in `docs/legal/data-handling.md`.
8. **Changes.** The operator may publish a new AUP/ToS version; continued use after notice constitutes acceptance for that org.
